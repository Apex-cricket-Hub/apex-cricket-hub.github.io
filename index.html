🆕 New organiser guide Optional • tap to hide
Follow these steps if this is your first tournament. Experienced organisers can collapse this guide.
  1. Tournament identity: add your tournament name, banner and team/player logos.
  2. Participants: enter the players or teams taking part.
  3. Competition format: choose your league/group structure, qualification and playoff method.
  4. Scoring rules: confirm overs, wickets and points used by your tournament.
  5. Tournament access PIN 🔐: create your own private PIN. This is mandatory. You will need it to manage this tournament later.
  6. Review & create: check your settings, then launch the tournament.
How to manage it later: long-press the APEX CRICKET HUB header to open Organiser Access, select your tournament and enter its PIN. Keep your PIN private and safe.

3 · Full scorecard International-style 10/11-a-side. Full batting & bowling card. Team totals for result & NRR.

Any number of groups is supported. Example: 12 players → 3 groups → 4/4/4. If a later stage has 9 players and 2 groups, use 4/5 or manual assignment.
